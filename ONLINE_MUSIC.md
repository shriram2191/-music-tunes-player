# Music Tunes online streaming

The Online tab streams full songs from **Audius** (free, legal, artist-uploaded
music) with no API key required. If the Audius network is unreachable, the app
automatically falls back to Internet Archive's `opensource_audio` collection.

## Country & language
Tap the \ud83c\udf10 icon in the Online search bar to pin one or more
country/language markets (e.g. India/Hindi + Korea/Korean). The app auto-detects
your market from the phone locale on first run and shows a regional chart row
per pinned market.

## Offline playback
Use the track \u22ee menu \u2192 Download on any online track to cache it on the
device. Cached tracks play without an internet connection.

## Notes
- Audius hosts rotate; the app re-resolves a healthy host automatically.
- Keep store-listing wording as "offline playback", not "download music",
  to stay within Audius terms and Play policy.
